Telegram Crypto Wallet

Store, receive, send, and manage crypto assets inside Telegram with a simple workflow.

The Crypto Bot wallet gives you a fully functional TON wallet inside Telegram — no separate app, no extra registration, and no friction. Whether you are new to crypto or an experienced user, you can store assets, generate receive addresses, send funds to contacts, and connect your balance to P2P trades and exchange flows, all from inside your existing Telegram account.

What the wallet helps you do

The Crypto Bot wallet is designed for people who want a fast and practical way to use crypto directly inside Telegram. Instead of switching between multiple apps, you can manage balances, receive funds, and move assets in one familiar interface. This is especially valuable if you already use Telegram for daily communication — your crypto workflow lives in the same place.

The wallet acts as a foundation for everything else in Crypto Bot. When you buy crypto through P2P, the funds land in your wallet. When you place an exchange order, the wallet provides the balance. When a customer pays an invoice, the payment arrives in your wallet. It is the central hub of your Telegram crypto experience.

  • Receive crypto by sharing your deposit address or QR code
  • Send crypto to any supported address or Telegram username
  • Use wallet funds together with P2P and Exchange flows
  • Stay inside Telegram for everyday crypto actions

How to use the Telegram crypto wallet — step by step

Getting started with the Crypto Bot wallet is straightforward. There is no separate sign-up process. Your Telegram account is your identity, and the bot handles the rest.

  1. Open Crypto Bot in Telegram — Use the official link or search for the bot. Tap Start to begin.
  2. Navigate to Wallet — Select the Wallet option from the main menu. You will see your current balance and available assets.
  3. Add funds — Tap Deposit to generate your receive address. Share the address or QR code with anyone sending you crypto. For first-time users, you can also buy crypto via P2P directly within the bot.
  4. Send crypto — Tap Send, enter the recipient address or Telegram username, specify the amount, and confirm. Your balance will update once the transaction is processed.
  5. Monitor your balance — Check the wallet screen at any time to see your holdings across all supported assets.
  6. Move to exchange or P2P — When you want to trade or sell, move seamlessly into the exchange or P2P flow directly from the wallet menu.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download anything to use the wallet?

No. The Crypto Bot wallet runs entirely inside Telegram. As long as you have Telegram installed, you have access. There is no separate wallet app to download or install.

Do I need a separate account or email to register?

No. Your Telegram account is all you need. The bot does not require a separate email, password, or registration process to get started.

Which cryptocurrencies does the wallet support?

The wallet supports TON and a range of other major crypto assets. Check the bot directly for the current list of supported assets, as coverage may be updated over time.

How do I receive crypto into my Telegram wallet?

Open the bot, go to Wallet, select the asset you want to receive, and tap Deposit. You will get a receive address and QR code you can share with the sender. Funds will appear in your balance after on-chain confirmation.

Can I send crypto to other Telegram users?

Yes. If the recipient also uses Crypto Bot, you can send crypto directly to their Telegram username without needing to copy a long wallet address. This makes sending simple and fast.

Is my wallet balance connected to P2P and Exchange?

Yes. Your wallet balance is used across wallet transfers, P2P trades, and exchange orders within Crypto Bot. Funds flow between these features without requiring manual transfers between separate accounts.

Is it safe to store crypto in the Telegram wallet?

The wallet includes built-in security mechanisms, but user-level security practices matter. Enable Telegram two-factor authentication, never share your account credentials, and always access the bot through the official Crypto Bot link. See the Security page for more details.
