Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-19

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how cryptobot.ltd (“Crypto Bot”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use this website. This website is designed to provide informational content, guides, tutorials, and referral links related to crypto services and Telegram-based tools.

We do not directly collect sensitive personal information such as passport data, private wallet keys, payment card information, or crypto account credentials through this website.

We may collect limited non-sensitive information such as:

  • basic analytics data such as page views, browser type, and device type;
  • technical logs used for performance, security, and anti-abuse purposes;
  • contact information voluntarily submitted through contact forms or communication channels.

Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, understand traffic, and analyze website performance. We may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics or similar services to understand how visitors use the website.

This website contains affiliate or referral links. If you click on these links and register or use third-party services, we may receive a commission or referral reward. These links do not increase the price you pay, but they may help support the operation of this website.

Third-Party Services

We do not control third-party platforms, apps, bots, wallets, exchanges, or payment providers that may be linked from this website. Any transactions, registrations, wallet actions, payments, trades, or other activities performed on third-party platforms are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

How We Use Information

Any information we collect is used only for legitimate website-related purposes, including:

  • maintaining and improving the website;
  • analyzing performance and user interaction;
  • responding to inquiries or support requests;
  • preventing abuse, spam, or malicious activity.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the website, such as hosting, analytics, or security providers, only to the extent necessary for those services to function.

Data Retention

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the website, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights related to access, correction, or deletion of personal data. If you want to request this, please contact us using the contact page on this website.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational steps to protect the website and any limited data processed through it. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children’s Privacy

This website is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law to use crypto-related or financial services in their jurisdiction.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact page.

How analytics data is used

Analytics data collected through this website is used primarily to understand how visitors navigate content, which pages attract the most interest, and where usability improvements could be made. This data is aggregated and anonymised where possible and is not used to build individual user profiles or target users with advertising.

If you prefer to avoid analytics tracking, most modern browsers offer options to block tracking scripts through privacy settings, extensions, or built-in tracking protection. Opting out of analytics does not affect your ability to use this website or access any of its content.

Cookie usage in more detail

Cookies used on this website fall into several general categories. Session cookies help maintain a consistent experience during a single visit. Analytics cookies collect non-identifiable information about how the website is used. Affiliate or referral cookies are used to track whether a user arrived via a referral link so that any applicable commission can be attributed correctly.

We do not use cookies to collect financial account data, wallet credentials, private keys, or other sensitive crypto-related information. The website itself does not provide account management functionality. Cookies used here are limited in scope to website analytics and referral attribution.

Third-party privacy policies

When you click affiliate or referral links on this website and land on a third-party platform, that platform's own privacy policy governs the collection and use of your data from that point onwards. We do not control, access, or receive the personal data you submit to those platforms.

Third-party services such as Google Analytics, hosting providers, and content delivery networks may also process limited technical data as part of their standard operations. These services maintain their own privacy policies and data processing agreements. We select service providers that implement appropriate data protection standards and use your data only to deliver services to us.

Depending on your location, applicable data protection laws may give you certain rights over data we hold about you. These may include the right to access what data we hold, the right to correct inaccuracies, the right to request deletion, or the right to object to certain types of processing.

Because this website collects very limited personal data — primarily basic analytics and optional contact form submissions — most data rights requests will be straightforward to address.
