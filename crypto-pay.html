How to accept crypto payments — step by step

Setting up your first crypto payment invoice through Crypto Bot is quick. No technical configuration is required for basic invoice creation.

  1. Open Crypto Bot in Telegram — Start the bot and navigate to the Crypto Pay section.
  2. Create an invoice — Select the asset, enter the amount, and add an optional description for the payment. This creates a shareable invoice link.
  3. Share the invoice link — Send the invoice link to your customer, client, or community members. They click the link and are taken directly to the payment interface inside Telegram.
  4. Customer completes payment — The customer opens the invoice, confirms the amount and asset, and approves the payment from their own Crypto Bot wallet or compatible wallet.
  5. Funds arrive in your wallet — Once the payment is confirmed, the funds arrive in your Crypto Bot wallet. You receive a notification inside Telegram.
  6. Manage and move funds — From your wallet, you can hold the received crypto, swap it to another asset via the exchange, or use it in other Crypto Bot flows.

Why invoices and payment links matter for crypto acceptance

One of the biggest friction points in accepting crypto is the manual process of sharing wallet addresses. When a customer wants to pay you in crypto, asking them to copy a long hex address, choose the correct network, and send the exact amount introduces multiple opportunities for error. A single mistake — wrong network, wrong address, wrong amount — can result in lost funds.

Invoices and payment links solve this by encoding all the payment details into a single shareable link. The customer clicks the link, sees the pre-filled amount and asset, and approves the payment in one step. There is no address to copy, no amount to calculate, and no network to select manually. The structured flow reduces errors and creates a better experience for both sender and receiver.

For businesses collecting many payments, this also means less time spent manually reconciling who paid what. Each invoice can include a reference or description, making it easier to match payments to specific orders or clients.
